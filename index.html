What it does

Takes a file or a paste

Drop in a PDF, or paste the lyrics straight into the box — chords and all. Either way you get the same slides. Each song’s Copy text gives you exactly what ProPresenter’s text import wants: a blank line starts a slide, [Chorus 1] names a group.

Reads the real layout

Song sheets are usually two columns. Text is rebuilt from glyph positions and read left column top-to-bottom, then right — not in whatever order the PDF happens to store it.

Strips the chords

A line is chords when every token on it is one. The grammar is strict, so All, And, Grace and Christ stay in the lyrics while F#m7b5 and C|C|D|G/B| go.

Rebuilds the lines

Syllable breaks are rejoined (for-gives → forgives), chord-alignment padding collapses, and long lines split into two balanced halves that stay on one slide.

Keeps the structure

Verse, Chorus and Bridge become ProPresenter groups with their usual colours, and an arrangement plays them back in the order the sheet prints — repeats included.

Output is import-ready text you can copy straight into ProPresenter, or a ProPresenter .pro document (version 7 and later, including 18 and 21). Built with pdf.js. Source on GitHub.
